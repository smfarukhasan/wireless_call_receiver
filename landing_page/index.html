Android accessibility utility

Answer calls with Bluetooth controls.

Wireless Call Receiver is designed primarily for blind and visually impaired users. It helps control supported cellular and internet calls with configured headset buttons while keeping sensitive processing on the device.

How accessibility works Read the privacy policy

Local by design

No developer data collection
No advertising or analytics SDK
No Android Internet permission

Permissions remain under your control in Android Settings.

Focused functionality

Built around a narrow, understandable call-control purpose

1

Connect

Detect a paired Bluetooth audio device after you grant Nearby Devices access.

2

Detect

Identify supported incoming calls through call interfaces and call-style notifications you explicitly allow.

3

Control

Invoke a supported Answer or End Call action from configured hardware controls or an optional deterministic auto-answer rule.

Your choice

No bundled permission surprise

Each runtime permission is requested separately. Accessibility and Notification Access each have a standalone in-app disclosure with Agree and Not Now choices before Android Settings opens.
