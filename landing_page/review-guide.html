App Reviewer Guide

A concise walkthrough of Wireless Call Receiver's core accessibility feature, permissions, and connected-device foreground service.

Core purpose

The app is designed primarily for blind and visually impaired users who need a Bluetooth hardware control to answer or end supported incoming calls without locating an on-screen control.

Review flow

  1. Launch the app and select a language.
  2. Open the Setup Wizard. Each runtime permission appears in a separate card.
  3. Select Accessibility Service. Review the standalone disclosure, test both Not Now and Agree and Continue, then enable Wireless Call Receiver in Android Settings.
  4. Select Notification Access. Review its separate disclosure and consent choices, then enable access if testing supported internet calls.
  5. Connect a Bluetooth headset and enable the Master Service.
  6. Place a supported test call. Use a configured headset control, or explicitly enable Auto-Answer to demonstrate the deterministic rule.
  7. Turn off the Master Service or disconnect Bluetooth to verify monitoring stops.

AccessibilityService scope

The service filters accessibility events and window roots to supported calling packages. It can read the labels and structure needed to identify a call action and can perform a targeted click or exact-bounds tap. It observes configured hardware controls while the Master Service is enabled.

Notification Listener scope

The listener ignores unsupported packages and does no processing while the Master Service is off. For supported call apps, it uses package, category, action label, and temporary native action-token information. Message bodies are not collected or transmitted.

Foreground service

The connectedDevice foreground service provides immediate, user-visible Bluetooth hardware monitoring while the Master Service is enabled. Its status notification identifies the active service. The task cannot be deferred during a live call-control session without breaking hardware-button responsiveness.

Data and access

No sign-in, account, subscription, paywall, or region restriction is present. The app has no Internet permission, ads, or analytics. See the Privacy Policy and Accessibility Statement.
